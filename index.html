HUMAN–COMPUTER INTERACTION & HAPTICS

Touch, perception,
and virtual experiences.

I study how people perceive and interact with digital experiences through touch.

My research interests include multimodal haptic feedback, haptic interaction in virtual reality, and LLMs for automatic haptic generation.
