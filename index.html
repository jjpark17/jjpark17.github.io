HUMAN–COMPUTER INTERACTION & HAPTICS

I am a Ph.D. student in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at Pohang University of Science and Technology, advised by Prof. Seungmoon Choi. My research focuses on human perception and interaction through touch, including multimodal haptic feedback (vibrotactile, thermal, and kinesthetic), haptic interaction in virtual reality, and LLMs for automatic haptic generation.
